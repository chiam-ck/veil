What needs review?

Review required means a sanitised output exists with specific tasks to check. It does not mean someone reviewed it, or that remaining personal data was definitely found.

Completed means supported processing finished without an actionable flag. Detection and OCR can still miss personal data. Information notices do not imply a review task.

Information, without automatic review

  • Saved PowerPoint printer settings removed from the redacted copy. Slides and layout are preserved; select a printer normally when printing.
  • Exactly uniform blank thumbnails and images.
  • Empty standard Office bibliography and recognized schema metadata.
  • Formulas that already lacked cached results in the source.
  • Supported OCR text without observed quality flags.
  • Plain stroked PDF borders/table rules and annotation icons already removed from output.

Actionable review tasks

  • Nonblank images where OCR recognizes no text.
  • Recognized lines with confidence below 0.8, text height below 12 input pixels or rotation above 10 degrees.
  • Unsupported scripts, unknown custom XML, extended comment schemas or retained Word/Excel printer settings that Veil cannot inspect.
  • Formula caches invalidated because redacted data affected calculations.
  • Conflicting replacement identifiers across tracked revision views.

The result identifies the affected page, cell or opaque Office part and the action to take. Information appears separately in a collapsed section.

Important limits

OCR thresholds are heuristics, not calibrated probabilities. High confidence for recognized lines cannot prove that every text region was recognized or that every personal detail was detected. A nonblank image is not assumed harmless just because it resembles a logo.

Unsupported executable/embedded content, unsafe formulas and known PII residuals still block output. Blocked and failed jobs provide reports only.

Historical jobs keep their original status. Resubmit the original to apply the current criteria; refreshing the page does not reprocess a document.
